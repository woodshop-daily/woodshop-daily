Shop notes

Hearing protection: Table saws, routers and sanders routinely exceed NIOSH’s recommended exposure limit of 85 dBA. Prefer quieter setups and shorter exposure first, then wear properly fitted earplugs or earmuffs — and dual protection (plugs + muffs) when levels stay very high. Source: CDC/NIOSH — Provide Hearing Protection.

Build Tips

1. Mark every mortise-and-tenon line from the same face. With a single-cutter marking gauge, scribe one cheek on all mortises and tenons from the marked reference face, then reset and scribe the other cheek the same way — joints stay aligned even when stock thickness varies.

Fine Woodworking — For Accurate Joints, Always Mark from the Same Face (Garrett Hack)

2. (Staining/finish coloring) Wipe stain on — don’t brush it. Flood a soaking-wet cloth, wipe the excess before it dries, and save the brush for recesses only. Fast-drying water-based, dye and lacquer stains blotch if you brush slowly on large panels.

Popular Woodworking — Wipe, Don’t Brush Stain (Bob Flexner)
